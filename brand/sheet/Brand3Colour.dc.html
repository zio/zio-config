Tints and shades
Each scale runs from a light wash to a deep shade. The core orange-red is step 500. Use 50 to 200 for fills and highlights. Step 700 (#A93816) is the orange-red for text on a light surface: it passes 4.5:1 where the core colour does not. Ink step 900 is the core ink.

Contrast pairs
Ratios are computed from the hex values (WCAG 2.x). Orange-red on white is 3.53:1, under the 4.5:1 text bar: use it for the middle bar of the symbol, rules and large graphics, and use step 700 for any orange-red text on light. White text on orange-red also fails, so labels on orange-red are ink.
