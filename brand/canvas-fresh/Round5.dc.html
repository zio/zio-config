Toggles, all horizontal, still a Z

No diagonal line at all. Every part is a horizontal switch with a round knob, like a settings screen; the Z comes from a full top, a full bottom, and shorter switches stepping between them.

1 Stair

Says configuration: Four switches, all horizontal. The two middle ones step from right to left, so the stack draws the Z's diagonal with nothing but switches.

Risk: the stair is coarse, and four bars can read as a menu.

2 Three switches

Says configuration: Three switches, all horizontal: full top, short coloured middle, full bottom. Knobs step right, centre, left and trace the diagonal.

Risk: the cleanest and the plainest; the Z comes mostly from the knobs and is weaker at 16 px.

3 Fine stair

Says configuration: Five switches. Top and bottom full, three short ones stepping between them for a smoother Z.

Risk: thin bars: busy at 24 px and below.
