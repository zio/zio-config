ZIO Config logo, fresh start, round 7

Lockup and favicon

Bold stair from round 6. Wordmark is Sora (ZIO bold, Config regular), converted to outlines so it needs no font. Files are in brand/final/ in the repo.

Lockup. Mark 64 high, wordmark cap height 30, gap 20 (units of the 64 box). Stacked version centres the mark over the wordmark.
Clear space. One bar height (14 of 64 units, about a fifth of the mark) on all sides.
Small sizes. Mark alone from 16 px (small cut, bars slightly thicker); horizontal lockup from 24 px high.
Favicon. Dark tile so it works on light and dark browser chrome. 16 px is a pixel-grid version without knobs; 32 px and up keep the knobs. ICO holds 16, 32, 48; 180, 192 and 512 px PNGs are full square.
