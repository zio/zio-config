ZIO Config logo, temporary design, round 9

The Z of ZIO, drawn as the stair

Temporary. The stair mark sits in the wordmark as the Z, so the logo is one piece: stair + IO + Config. Nothing in brand/final/ changes unless you accept this. Compare with the standalone mark and lockup on the earlier boards.

1 Square Z

The stair at full cap height replaces the Z. Same proportions as the mark.

Risk: reads wide next to the narrow I; the Z reads a little like an equals sign with a tail.

2 Narrow Z

Bars shortened to about 78% so the stair is letter-width. Closest to a real Z next to I and O.

Risk: knobs get tight; still lighter than the bold stems of I and O.

3 One colour

Narrow Z with all bars in ink, for one-colour use. Knobs still cut out.

Risk: loses the orange-red that makes the stair look like a Z; reads as three lines.

My read: it works as a wordmark idea, but the stair is lighter than the bold I and O, and at cap height it reads as a Z mostly because you already know the word. Version 2 is the one to judge. If the Z is not clear enough for you, the mark beside "ZIO Config" (the current lockup) stays the safer choice.
