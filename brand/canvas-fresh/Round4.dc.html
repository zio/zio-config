ZIO Config logo, fresh start, round 4

Toggles, drawn as a Z

From round 3 concept 2. Top and bottom switches are the Z's bars, a diagonal joins them, knobs sit where the Z turns. Three colour treatments of the same shape.

1 Ink pills, colour diagonal

Says configuration: Two toggles at the top and bottom, a coloured diagonal between them. Z first, switch second.

Risk: the diagonal has no knob of its own, so it reads more as a Z than as three switches.

2 All ink, colour knobs

Says configuration: Three switches in one ink, with coloured knobs at the two corners and the middle. The knobs are the only colour.

Risk: busy: three knobs on one Z; the middle knob is small at 16 px.

3 Colour pills, ink diagonal

Says configuration: Top and bottom toggles in colour, ink diagonal joining them. Strongest colour, most playful.

Risk: the colour sits on the horizontals, so the diagonal looks like a connector rather than part of the Z.
