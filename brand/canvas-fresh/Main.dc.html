ZIO Config logo, fresh start

Five directions, drawn from scratch

No brand rules and none of the earlier rounds. I looked at what the library does: it describes configuration as values, reads it from many sources, and merges them. Colour is a plain ink and one orange-red, only so you can judge the shapes. Each concept is shown in ink and in colour, light and dark, with 16, 24 and 32 px below.

1 Dial

A knob with a pointer and ticks: a value being tuned. Below 32 px the ticks drop and only the knob and pointer remain.

Risk: reads as a volume knob, a familiar settings image.

2 Braces

A heavy Z held between two curly braces: the config file and the library in one glyph.

Risk: three strokes compete at 16 px, and braces are a common developer symbol.

3 Layers

Three see-through sheets. Where they overlap the colour deepens: sources merged, the front one wins.

Risk: generic as a shape, and it relies on transparency, so one-colour print needs a flat version.

4 z.

A lowercase z with a coloured full stop, from the package path zio.config. Shown in a stock font; it would be redrawn.

Risk: the simplest and most wordmark-like, but says little about configuration.

5 Switch board

A 5 by 5 grid of dots. The ones switched on draw a Z; the rest stay faint: flags and settings, on and off.

Risk: the dots are 2 px at 16 px, and it looks like a bitmap font.

Colour check. Orange-red #F2501F is 3.53:1 on white and #FF6B3D is 6.45:1 on the dark ground #101521, so the marks pass the 3:1 graphics bar. For orange-red text on white a darker step would be needed.
