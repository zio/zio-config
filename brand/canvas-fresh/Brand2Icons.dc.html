Brand sheet · 02 · Icon and favicon

One mark, every small place

The icon is the symbol on a rounded tile. The tile is what lets one file work on a light tab, a dark tab, a phone home screen and a GitHub avatar. The browser tab is the exception: it uses the bars alone, with no tile, redrawn on a 16 px grid and switching colour with the tab theme.
2.1
App icon
Dark tile is the default. The light tile is for dark backgrounds that would swallow the dark one. Corner radius is 22.5% of the side; platforms that apply their own mask (iOS, Android adaptive) use the full-bleed square, with the symbol inside the central 80% safe circle.

favicon.svg is the tab icon: the same three bars, no tile, ink and orange-red on a light tab, snow and light orange-red on a dark tab (it follows the browser theme). The dark tile is kept for the ICO fallback, apple-touch-icon and app icons.
The tab icon is drawn by hand on the 16 px grid: three 4 px bars, 2 px gaps and a 2 px round knob cut out of each bar. The dark tile vanished on dark tabs, so the tab icon drops it.

2.4
Profile avatar
Avatars are cropped to a circle or a rounded square. The symbol sits inside the central 80%, so neither crop touches the bars.
2.5
One-colour versions
For stamps, engraving, fax and single-ink print. All bars take one colour and the knobs stay as holes. The one-colour orange-red is for the symbol only, never for text: it is 3.53:1 on white.
