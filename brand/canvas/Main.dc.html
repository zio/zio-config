ZIO Config logo, round 1 of 3 or so

Five ways to draw a setting

Each concept is one solid glyph with one accent element, shown on light and dark. Colour is a new family hue (plum with a lime accent), not terracotta, mint, ultramarine or amber. Type is Sora with DM Mono.

A

Switch

A setting turned on. Plum track, lime knob.

Accent: the knob.

Risk: reads as a stock UI toggle. The pill is only 10 px tall at 16 px.

B

Key = value

A dot for the key, two bars for the value, like port = 8080.

Accent: the key dot.

Risk: the two bars echo ZIO Logging's lines. Short and wide, so it has the least presence.

C

Cascade

Default, environment, override. The front layer wins.

Accent: the front layer.

Risk: the back layers blur into one block under 24 px. Nearest rival is ZIO Blocks.

D

Branch

A root key with three child keys: the shape of a config tree.

Accent: one leaf, the overridden value.

Risk: thin strokes at 16 px, and it can read as a molecule.

E

Fold Z

A Z ribbon with slanted ends. The diagonal is the override path.

Accent: the diagonal.

Risk: nearest to Prelude (Z with accent) and Blocks. The only Z, so the only one that sits inline in the lockup.

Recommendation

A, Switch. It is the only concept that says "configuration" with no explanation, it is distinct from all four existing marks, and its clear space and small cut are simple. E is the fallback if you want a Z. Next round: narrow to two, build lockups, small cuts and the favicon.
