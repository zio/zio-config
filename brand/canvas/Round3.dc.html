Round 3

Four Zs with a bit of character

A clean page, none of the earlier concepts. New shapes and two new palettes: Poppy (red with an ink accent) and Orchid (fuchsia with a deep teal accent). Pink, violet, cyan, plum and lime from earlier rounds are out. Light on top, dark below, with the small sizes underneath.

1 Plug Z

One rounded cable bent into a Z, ending in a plug. Settings you plug in. Accent: the plug.

Risk: the most character, but the prongs shrink to a dot at 16 px and it is a literal electrical plug.

4 Italic Z

A heavy Z leaning forward, with barely softened corners. Single colour, no accent.

Risk: the plainest and sturdiest, but the least playful, and still close to Prelude's solid Z.

Contrast. Mark colours are graphics, which need 3:1 against the page. Poppy #E5242F on white 4.54, dark #FF6070 on #130F1C 6.43. Orchid #C026D3 on white 4.71, dark #E879F9 7.67. Accents: ink 17.87, deep teal #00606A 7.29, dark ink #F3EFF8 16.64, dark teal #2DD4E0 10.43. Link and button text on light uses deeper steps: Poppy #B3141F 6.92, Orchid #9A1AAB 6.75, both above 4.5. Accent against brand inside the mark: Poppy 3.93 light and 2.59 dark; Orchid 1.55 light and 1.36 dark, so Orchid's two parts separate by hue only.
