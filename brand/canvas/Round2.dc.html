4 Chunky Z

A heavy solid Z with softened corners. No accent: single colour.

Risk: closest to Prelude's solid Z. Best at 16 px.

Contrast: mark colours are graphics, so they need 3:1 against the page. Light: pink 4.38, cyan 3.30, violet 6.47 (accents 3.36, 3.90, 5.48). Link and button text uses a deeper step: pink #B3125F 6.63, violet #5B21C9 8.38, cyan #00718F 5.59, all above 4.5 on white. Dark marks and text pass 4.5 everywhere (6.57 to 10.96). Accent against brand is 1.24 to 1.70, so the two halves separate by hue only.
